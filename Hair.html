Explore hair growth ideas with natural ingredients and how to prepare them.

1,Rosemary Scalp Stimulant

Ingredients

  • 2-3 fresh rosemary springs
  • 2 cups water

Preparation

  1. Boil the water and add rosemary springs
  2. Let it simmer for 5 minutes the cool completely
  3. Use as a final rinse after washing your hair
  4. Do not rinse if off with water afterwards

2,Fenugreek Strength Paste

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon of fenugreek seeds
  • 1 tablespoon coconunt oil

Preparation

  1. Soak seeds overnight and grind into a paste
  2. Mix with oil
  3. Apply for 30 minutes then rinse

3,Baking Soda Exfoilating Scrub

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon baking soda

Preparation

  1. Wet your and rub the baking soda directly into the scalp
  2. Leave for 2 minutes the rinse thoroughly with water only(skip shampoo this time)
  3. Hair may feel dry at first but will produce natural oils soon

4,Egg And Yogurt Strengthening

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoon yogurt

Preparation

  1. Whisk together and apply to damp hair
  2. Leave for 3o minutes
  3. Rinse with COLD water then shampoo

5,Honey And Milk Softner

  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1/2 cup milk
  1. Mix honey into milk and pour over hair after washing
  2. Massage and leave for 15 minutes
  3. Rinse with lukewarm water

6,Tomato Mask

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe tomato
  • Preparation
    1. Puree the tomato and apply it to the scalp
    2. Leave it for 20 minutes then wash with lukewarm water
    3. Repeat it twice a week

    7,Avocado-mayo Smoothness

    Ingredients

    • 1/2 avocado
    • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise

    Preparation

    1. Mash and mix into a creamy paste
    2. Apply to hair for 30 minutes
    3. Wash thoroughly twice with shampoo

    8,Black Sesame Mask

    Ingredients

    • 2 tablespoon black sesame oil

    Preparation

    1. Apply oil to the scalp
    2. Massage for 10 minutes then wrap in warm towel
    3. Leave for 30 minutes then wash

    9,Cooling Peppermint Mist

    Ingredients

    • 3 drops of peppermint oil
    • 1/2 cup water
    1. Mix in a spray bottle
    2. Spray on itchy areas as needed for instant relief

    10,Castor And Coconunt Density Mask

    Ingredients

    • 2 tablespoon castor oil
    • 2 tablespoon coconunt oil

    Preparation

    1. Mix the oils and warm them slightly
    2. Apply to the scalp and hair from roots to tips
    3. Wrap hair in warm towel for 1 hour then wash
    4. Repeat once or twice a week
