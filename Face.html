9,Coffee And Coconut Antioxigant Scrub

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons of ground coffe
  • 2 tablespoons of coconut oil

Preparation

  1. Mix coffee ground and oil into a smooth paste
  2. Rub onto the face using fingers or a brush a brush in circular motions
  3. Leave for 20-30 minutes
  4. Rinse with water and pat dry
  5. Repeat 1-2 times per week

10,Potato Juice Skin Brightener

  • 1 fresh potato

Preparation

  1. Grate a raw potato and squeeze out the juice
  2. Apply the juice to the the face using a cotton ball
  3. Leave it for 20 minutes until dry
  4. Rinse with cool water
  5. Repeat daily for the best results
