4,Sugar And Yeast 7-day Whitening

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup coarse white sugar
  • 2 tablespoon instant yeast
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Preparation

  1. Mix sugar,yeast,and lemon juice
  2. Gradually add olive oil to form a paste
  3. Massage onto wet skin for 15 minutes
  4. Wash with lukewarm water and dry
  5. Repeat daily for one week
