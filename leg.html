This page will tell how to take care of your cracked heels and how to keep your feet smooth

1,Banana And Cocount Softening Balm

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 tablespoon coconunt oil

Preparation

  1. Mash the banana and mix with coconunt oil until creamy
  2. Apply the paste to clean heels and cover with socks
  3. Leave for 20-30 minutes
  4. Rinse with warm water and scrub gently with a pumice stone

2,Rice Flour Heel Scrub

Ingredients

  • Handful of ground rice
  • Few teaspoon honey
  • Few drops apple cider vinegar
  • Few drops apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon of almond oil

Preparation

  1. Soak feet in warm water for 10 minutes
  2. Mix ingredients into a thick paste
  3. Rub paste onto heels and massage well
  4. Repeat several times a week

3,Acidic Vaseline Softener

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon vaseline
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Preparation

  1. Rub feet with a pumice stone then wash and dry
  2. Mix vasline and lemon juice
  3. Apply to feet and massage until absorbed
  4. Repeat daily before bed

4,Honey Foot Bath

Ingredients

  • 1 cup honey
  • Warm water

Preparation

  1. Fill a basin with warm water and mix in the honey
  2. Soak feet for 15-20 minutes
  3. Gently scrub andf dry
  4. Repeat daily or 3 times a week

5,Coconunt Overnight Shield

Ingredients

  • Coconunt oil
  • Clean sock

Preparation

  1. Soak feet in warm water and clean off dead skin
  2. Dry thoroughly and massage with coconunt oil
  3. Wear clean socks and leave overnight

6,Shea-lemon Healing Balm

Ingredients

  • Shea butter
  • Fresh lemon juice

Preparation

  1. Soak and gently scrub feet
  2. Dry and massage with a blend of shea butter and lemon juice
  3. Wear socks and leave overnight

7,Mouthwash Skin Softener

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mouthwash
  • 1 cup white vinegar
  • 2 cups water

Preparation

  1. Mix in abasin and soak feet for 10-15 minutes
  2. Scrub with a pumice stone
  3. Rinse,dry and moisturize
  4. Repeat daily for 3-4 days

8,Baking Soda Foot Exfoliant

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoon baking soda
  • Warm water

Preparation

  1. Dissolve baking soda in warm water
  2. Soak feet foe 10-15 minutes
  3. Scrub with a pumice stone then rinse
  4. Repeat twice a week

9,Aloe Collagen Repair

Ingredients

  • Aloe vera gel
  • Warm water
  • Socks

Preparation

  1. Soak feet in warm water and dry
  2. Apply a thick layer of aloe gel
  3. Wear soks overnight
  4. Repeat daily for 4-5 days

10,Paraffin Smoothing Shield

Ingredients

  • Parafin wax
  • Coconunt oil

Preparation

  1. Melt wax with coconunt oil(ensure it is safe temperature)
  2. Dip feet into the mixture several times to form a coat
  3. Wrap feet into the mixture several times to form a coat
  4. Peel off wax
