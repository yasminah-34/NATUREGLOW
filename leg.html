this page will tell how to take care of your cracked heels and how to keep your feet smooth

1,banana and cocount softening balm

ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 tablespoon coconunt oil

preparation

  1. mash the banana and mix with coconunt oil until creamy
  2. apply the paste to clean heels and cover with socks
  3. leave for 20-30 minutes
  4. rinse with warm water and scrub gently with a pumice stone

2,rice flour heel scrub

  • handful of ground rice
  • few teaspoon honey
  • few drops apple cider vinegar
  • few drops apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon of almond oil

preparation

  1. soak feet in warm water for 10 minutes
  2. mix ingredients into a thick paste
  3. rub paste onto heels and massage well
  4. repeat several times a week

3,acidic vaseline softener

ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon vaseline
  • juice of 1 lemon

preparation

  1. rub feet with a pumice stone then wash and dry
  2. mix vasline and lemon juice
  3. apply to feet and massage until absorbed
  4. repeat daily before bed

4,honey foot bath

ingredients

  • 1 cup honey
  • warm water

preparation

  1. fill a basin with warm water and mix in the honey
  2. soak feet for 15-20 minutes
  3. gently scrub andf dry
  4. repeat daily or 3 times a week

5,coconunt overnight shield

ingredients

  • coconunt oil
  • clean sock

preparation

  1. soak feet in warm water and clean off dead skin
  2. dry thoroughly and massage with coconunt oil
  3. wear clean socks and leave overnight
