Smooth hands

1,honey and sugar hand scrub

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon almod oil

Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients to create a scrub
  2. Gently massage over your hands for 3-5 minutes
  3. Rinse with lukewarm water
  4. Apply hand cream immediately after

2,Olive-salt Smoothing Scrub

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon salt
  • 2 tablespoon olive oil

Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients in a bowl
  2. Rub onto hands for upto 5 minutes
  3. Leave until dry(about 10 mins) then rinse with lukewarm water

3,Honey-lemon Softner

Ingredients

  • 1/2 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon natural honey

Preparation

  1. Mix well and rub onto hands for 2 minutes
  2. Leave for 5 minutes then rinse with warm water

4,Avocado-banana Hand Mask

Ingredients

  • 1 avocado
  • 1 fresh banana

Preparation

  1. Mash the banana and avocado together until smooth
  2. Spread over hands leave for 15 minutes
  3. Wash with cold water and soap

5,Overnight Vasline Therapy

Ingredients

  • Vasline
  • Cotton gloves

Preparation

  1. Apply a thick layer of vasline before bed
  2. Wear cotton gloves all night
  3. Wash hands in the morning

6,Yogurt Mask

Preparation

  • 1 tablespoon plain yogurt

Preparation

  1. Gently massage yogurt onto hands for 5 minutes
  2. Leave for 10 minutes then rinse with warm water
  3. Use daily or every other day

7,Banana-honey Mask

Ingredients

  • 1/2 banana (mashed)
  • Small amount of honey
  • Small amount of olive oil

Preparation

  1. Mix onto a paste and apply to hands
  2. Leave for 15-20 minutes
  3. Wash with warm water then cold water to lock in moisture

8,Wheat-yogurt Skin Smoother

  • 3 teaspoon plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon wheat flour

Ingredients

  1. Mix into a thick paste
  2. Apply to hands and leave until completely dry
  3. Rinse with warm water 1-2 times weekly

9,Soda Hand Polish

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoon honey
  • 2 teaspoon baking soda

Preparation

  1. Mix into a paste and massage onto hands for 1 minute
  2. Leave for 5 minutes then wash with warm water
  3. Repeat 1-2 times weekly

10,Glycerin-rose Hydrator

Ingredients

  • 75ml glycerin
  • 25ml rose water

Preparation

  1. Mix thoroughly and store in a bottle
  2. Apply at least once daily by rubbing onto hands
